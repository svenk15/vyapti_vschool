Classification is explicit: an **implementation defect** is behavior supported by an approved/current owner contract that renders or acts incorrectly; **missing visual evidence** means the supported behavior has not been directly compared; an **explicitly deferred feature** retains its recorded later owner task; and a **reference-less extension** is real supported behavior styled with established patterns but awaiting human visual review. Prototype-only reviewer controls are intentionally omitted under the execution-pack instructions and are not a product deviation. Synthetic session counts need not reproduce memory-only prototype counts.
