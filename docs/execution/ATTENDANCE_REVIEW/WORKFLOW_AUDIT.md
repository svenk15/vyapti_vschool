# Attendance workflow audit

Status terms: **implemented** means the current route and owner contract exist; **partial** means an approved outcome is narrower than the workflow reference; **missing** means the approved capability is absent. Functional evidence, current visual observation and human acceptance remain separate.

## Administrator versus other workspaces

- Teachers alone mark and submit assigned registers. The Administrator detail explicitly remains read-only until a permitted correction/cancellation action.
- Principals receive oversight, decision and Coordination projections only under their own grants. A Principal/Administrator role label does not grant explanation, correction-request or Coordination authority.
- Parents submit explanations and Parent correction requests only through current guardian relationship and purpose checks.
- Students submit self correction requests only through current student identity/purpose checks.

## Correctness boundaries retained in every proposal

1. **Branch-local dates:** scheduling, “today”, explanation eligibility and report periods use the branch timezone. Browser dates are candidates, never authority.
2. **Live scope:** account/token version, institution/branch state, membership, capability, release, entitlement, activation and resource relationships are rechecked server-side.
3. **Distinct states:** Scheduled, Open, Submitted and Cancelled have different actions and reporting meanings. Missing/unsubmitted is never converted to absent.
4. **Frozen history:** roster freezes when opened; submitted records and immutable revisions remain historical. Cancellation never deletes history.
5. **Source ownership:** Timetable owns publication/occurrence identity; Attendance owns session creation and guards. Trusted linkage is server-established.
6. **Correction versus explanation:** an accepted explanation records a disposition only. Marks change solely through the Attendance correction command.
7. **Recovery:** changed versions require fresh review. Unknown remains unknown and retains exact payload/key. Replay rechecks current authority before returning protected results.
8. **Committed write, failed refresh:** a confirmed receipt remains success; recovery performs a read and must not resubmit the mutation.
9. **Follow-up verification:** Coordination closure rechecks current source evidence and separation of duties; inbox visibility does not grant source authority.
