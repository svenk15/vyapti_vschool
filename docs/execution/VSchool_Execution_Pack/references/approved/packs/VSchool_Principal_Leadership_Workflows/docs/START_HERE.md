# Start here: choose a school activity

Open VSchool_Principal_Leadership_Workflows.html. Choose an activity from one of five groups. The guide shows one step at a time: who acts now, what is on their screen, what they need to do and what happens afterwards.

You can go back, review any step, return to the activity list and resume where you left off during the same visit. This is a walkthrough using fictional examples; it does not send messages or change school records.

## My day

Start with today’s priorities and the work waiting for you.

### Review my day

See today’s priorities and who is taking care of them.

1. **Choose the right school — Principal.** Confirm the branch before reviewing the day. Every item on the page belongs to that permitted branch.
2. **See what needs attention — Principal.** Open a relevant item rather than navigating through every module. The appropriate detail page explains the issue and available action.
3. **Decide the next action — Principal.** Decide whether to act, assign a follow-up or leave the item for its existing owner. A clear next action exists; the original source record stays with its responsible module.
4. **Check progress — Principal.** Return later to check what changed. The principal sees which items still need attention and which were verified as finished.

**Finished when:** The principal knows today’s priorities and who is handling each one.

## Attendance & pupil support

Check class attendance and help pupils who need attention.

### Find missing attendance

See which classes still need attendance and arrange a follow-up.

1. **Find missing attendance — Principal.** Select the 3 awaiting submissions. A list opens showing the exact classes, teachers and due times.
2. **Check the class — Principal.** Open the class and check whether another person is already following up. The principal knows which submission is missing and avoids creating duplicate work.
3. **Choose who follows up — Principal.** Choose coordinator Anita, set 11:30 as the due time and confirm. Anita receives an assigned work item. Ravi remains the teacher allowed to record this class’s attendance.
4. **Teacher submits attendance — Coordinator and teacher.** Anita follows up with Ravi. Ravi records and submits attendance in the teacher workspace. The received count becomes 13 of 15. The task says Ready to verify; two other submissions are still missing.
5. **Check and finish — Principal.** Open the submission and select Verify and close. This one follow-up is closed, with who checked it and when. The other two remain outstanding.

**Finished when:** The class attendance is submitted and an authorized person has checked it. Assigning the task alone is not completion.

### Follow up a pupil’s absence

Check the dates, contact the right guardian and agree a next step.

1. **Check the attendance dates — Principal.** Open Aarav’s permitted attendance history. Exact dates and class records are shown, including any missing teacher submissions.
2. **Read any explanation — Principal or authorized reviewer.** Check the records and existing school follow-up before contacting the family. An incomplete register is not mistaken for a pupil being absent.
3. **Contact the guardian — Authorized school staff.** Contact the verified guardian, or assign this to the school’s responsible staff member. The family conversation has an owner. A message being sent is shown separately from a reply.
4. **Review the agreed action — Responsible staff and principal.** Review the outcome on that date and arrange further support if needed. The communication or support action can be completed; attendance continues to be monitored separately.

**Finished when:** The school has contacted the appropriate guardian, recorded the agreed action and reviewed it. One reply does not prove the attendance problem is solved.

## Teaching & staff

Arrange cover and review where teaching support is needed.

### Arrange a cover teacher

Make sure a suitable teacher is assigned to the affected class.

1. **Find the class needing cover — Principal.** Open the affected class. The subject, time, room and current cover status are visible.
2. **Choose an available teacher — Authorized timetable coordinator.** Choose a suitable available teacher and prepare the assignment. The system checks the new teacher’s clashes and eligibility.
3. **Review the assignment — Principal, only if assigned as approver.** Approve the proposal, request a change or send it to the actual approver. The timetable module records the authorized cover assignment; leadership review alone does not change it.
4. **Teacher sees the class — Cover teacher.** Open the class and follow the permitted teaching and attendance flow. The class has a named teacher and the teacher can see their work.

**Finished when:** A valid cover assignment exists and the assigned teacher can access the class.

### Review class progress

Discuss incomplete work or delayed feedback and plan support.

1. **Choose a class — Principal.** Choose a class or subject to review. The principal sees the exact work behind the summary.
2. **Understand the situation — Principal and academic lead.** Inspect the situation with the responsible teacher. The discussion uses the right records rather than assuming low completion means poor teaching.
3. **Agree a support action — Academic lead or teacher.** Arrange the appropriate teaching support or permitted family follow-up. The action is tracked without changing marks or private teacher drafts.
4. **Review what changed — Principal.** Review what was done and whether further support is needed. The action’s completion and any change in learning outcomes are described separately.

**Finished when:** The agreed support action has been reviewed using current evidence. Completing a task is not a guarantee of improved learning.

## Families & notices

Handle parent concerns and review school communication.

### Respond to a parent concern

Make sure someone investigates, responds and follows through.

1. **Open the assigned concern — Principal or designated reviewer.** Open it only if assigned and permitted to review it. The reviewer sees the details appropriate to their role.
2. **Acknowledge and clarify — Reviewer.** Acknowledge receipt and ask for clarification when necessary. The family knows who is handling the concern and what happens next.
3. **Investigate the concern — Assigned investigator.** Gather the relevant information and record findings in the restricted case. The reviewer has enough information to decide an appropriate response.
4. **Review and send a response — Authorized reviewer.** Send the reviewed response and track the promised actions. Response delivery and completion of the actions are recorded separately.
5. **Check and close — Authorized reviewer.** Close according to the school process, retaining history and any escalation route. The concern has a documented outcome and can be reopened or escalated when warranted.

**Finished when:** The school has responded and completed the actions required by its complaint process. Sending a reply alone does not close the case.

### Review a school notice

Check the wording and audience before the notice is published.

1. **Notice is submitted — School administrator.** Submit it to the configured approver. The principal receives it only if the approval rules assign it to them.
2. **Review the notice — Principal.** Review the notice; return it with a comment if something needs changing. The administrator receives the requested change; the notice has not been published.
3. **Approve the wording — Principal.** Approve that revision if satisfied. The notice becomes approved. Publishing remains a separate authorized action in this proposed flow.
4. **Publish the notice — Authorized publisher.** Publish or schedule it through the communication module. Delivery results are tracked, including failures. Approved does not mean delivered.
5. **Check delivery — Principal or communication owner.** Assign follow-up where the school requires it. The school knows who still needs communication follow-up.

**Finished when:** The correct notice is published, delivery exceptions are handled, and any required acknowledgements are tracked.

## Reviews & reports

Bring together the facts and agree what happens next.

### Prepare a weekly summary

Review the facts and carry forward work that still needs attention.

1. **Choose the school and week — Principal.** Choose the week and confirm the branch you want to review. The summary uses that period and branch. Information you are not allowed to see is excluded.
2. **Check the facts — Principal.** Open an item that needs explanation and check the underlying records. You can distinguish completed work, unresolved issues and unavailable information.
3. **Agree the next actions — Principal and responsible staff.** Agree what happens next, who will do it and when it will be reviewed. Outstanding work has a clear owner. Preparing a summary does not mark that work completed.
4. **Review and save — Principal.** Review the summary and save or export it if you have permission. The saved summary includes its period and the time the information was last updated. Sharing with other people is a separate permitted action.

**Finished when:** The summary has been checked and saved, and outstanding actions have named owners. The actions themselves remain open until completed.

## Where to start

Choose **Attendance & pupil support → Find missing attendance**. This connects the principal, coordinator and teacher in one complete example.

**Teacher attendance submission** means the attendance recorded for a scheduled class. A **follow-up** is a work item assigned to someone with a due time. **Waiting for review** means the result is ready to check. **Completed** means the required work has been checked; it does not imply unrelated actions or the underlying problem have all been solved.

## For design and development

Design notes in the reader open the detailed 46-workflow reference. The five groups organize the experience; they do not change module ownership, permission checks or delivery phases. Physical data models, production screens and implementation remain separate work.
