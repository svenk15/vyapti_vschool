# Principal & School Leadership — product charter
Edition 01 · 8 October 2026 · PRODUCT_WORKFLOW_PROPOSAL

## Product purpose
Help a school leader answer: **What needs attention? What is the evidence? Who owns the next action? Has it been resolved?**

The leadership workspace is an orchestration and read experience over existing owner modules. The principal is accountable for oversight without becoming a superuser, attendance clerk, payroll operator or specialist clinician by default.

This catalogue defines 46 workflows across 12 areas. It covers the leadership needs of the existing VSchool phase blueprint and marks extensions. It does not claim every jurisdiction, school policy or specialist workflow is settled.

## Persona boundaries
| Persona | Primary purpose | Not implied |
|---|---|---|
| Branch principal | Branch oversight and specifically delegated decisions | All student/case/finance records |
| Institution leader | Explicitly granted multi-branch summaries | Automatic branch-detail access |
| Acting principal/deputy | Dated delegated scope | Permanent authority or self-delegation |
| School administrator | Operational source-record setup and correction | Every leadership/specialist decision |
| Specialist owner | Finance, HR, safeguarding, health or service purpose | General leadership visibility into private content |

One global account can hold several roles. Workspace, institution, branch and action authority remain explicit. Visibility = released AND institution-entitled AND branch-enabled AND current actor permitted. A licensed module can still be unavailable to this leader.

## Design principles
1. Every actionable number leads to scoped evidence, with definition, cutoff and completeness.
2. Missing information is not a negative outcome. Unsubmitted attendance is not absence; undelivered is not unread; approval is not settlement.
3. Assign ownership and an evidence-based finish condition. Reminders alone are not resolution.
4. A decision belongs to its source module. The central inbox never becomes a universal approval bypass.
5. Surface the next useful action; avoid a dashboard of decorative KPI cards.
6. Preserve context, unsaved work and uncertain outcomes. Reauthorize at execution and download.
7. Make sensitive-case boundaries visible through safe routing, without leaking case existence/content in broad summaries.
8. Use the shared theme, shell and components already defined for other VSchool workspaces.

## Phase alignment
The source persona packs map Phase 1 to school/academic/staff/student/attendance foundations; Phase 2 to family communication/requests/basic analytics; Phase 3 to timetable/learning and richer lifecycle; Phase 4 assessments; Phase 5 finance; Phase 6 independent services; Phase 7 reviewed AI. Current live Bible/repository may have newer decisions; reconcile before coding.

Leadership read-only attendance can build on Phase 1 records after an explicit leadership grant is designed. Internal follow-up coordination is a proposed Phase 2 extension. It is not secretly claimed to exist in Phase 1. Publication, staffing and specialist actions wait for their owner modules.

## Success measures
Measure whether leaders can find an overdue register, identify the responsible owner, distinguish stale from current evidence, assign one follow-up and verify the outcome. Operational measures: time to useful action, duplicate follow-ups prevented, proportion of closures backed by valid evidence, unresolved delivery failures, and time spent in an unknown command state. Establish baselines before setting targets. No teacher league tables, pupil risk scores or hidden behavioral ranking.
