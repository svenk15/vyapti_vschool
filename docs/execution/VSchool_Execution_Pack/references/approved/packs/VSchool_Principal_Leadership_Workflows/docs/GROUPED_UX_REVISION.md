# Grouped experience revision — 8 October 2026

Eight guided activities now sit under five familiar groups: My day, Attendance & pupil support, Teaching & staff, Families & notices, and Reviews & reports.

The starting view uses action-led labels. Each walkthrough shows one step at a time, who acts now, what to do and what happens afterwards. Step navigation, back, returning to the activity list and resuming during the same visit are supported. Completion screens explain the finish condition without claiming a real school action was performed.

Technical references, phase allocation and the 46 detailed workflows remain under Design notes. workflow-groups.json maps every detailed workflow to exactly one primary group. Backend ownership, authorization and release dependencies are preserved.

SIMPLIFIED_UX.md is the implementation contract for plain language, task states, handoffs, errors, module visibility and shared components. START_HERE.md explains the eight activities by group.

The earlier seven-story version is superseded for presentation. Product review with non-technical school staff remains pending. Automated reader checks passed; production implementation, database validation and native screen-reader verification are outside this artifact.
